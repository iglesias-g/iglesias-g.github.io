I'm a Physical Oceanographer who is interested in flow--topography and air--sea interactions. One of
my main interests is to understand how observed data can be reproduced using high-resolution models.

I recently finished my master's degree in Physical Oceanography at the University of Sao Paulo, and I'm
currently working as a research assistant at the National Institute for Space Research (INPE-Brazil), studying
the Polar Front and the Antarctic Circumpolar Current over the Drake Passage. Meanwhile, I'm also looking
abroad for PhD opportunities.

For an in-depth list of skills, conferences, publications, etc., please check my [curriculum
vitae](https://github.com/iglesias-g/iglesias-g.github.io/raw/master/assets/pdf/CV_Iglesias_Gabriel.pdf).
